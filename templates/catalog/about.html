The Genesis

Why We Built Youth Club Library

In late 2020, university graduates, scholars, and youth volunteers in Chattogram identified an acute crisis: while cafes, malls, and entertainment hubs were surging, quiet spaces dedicated to intellectual contemplation and classical Islamic literature were absent for young people.

Vital volumes of Tafsir, Hadith commentaries, Islamic history, and philosophical treatises were either priced beyond student budgets or locked in distant academic vaults.

Youth Club Library was created to make wisdom radically accessible. We opened our central Kazir Dewri branch with a simple principle: anyone can walk in, read freely, take notes, and find a community that values deep thinking over social feeds.

“A generation disconnected from its classical intellectual roots becomes easily swayed by superficial noise. Our library anchors young minds in sound knowledge and sincere purpose.”

— Founding Team, Youth Club Chittagong

Core Ecosystem

The Five Pillars of Youth Club Library

Every program is designed to eliminate friction between passionate readers and genuine knowledge.

Pillar 01

Free Walk-In Reading Room

Spend hours immersed in contemplation. Anyone can walk in, take any title from our shelves, and read without paying any admission fee.

No card or entry fee required ✓
Pillar 02

Borrow With Membership

Take books home for extended study. Our student and general memberships offer flexible borrowing limits, easy renewals, and zero hidden penalties.

Plans starting from ৳100/mo ✓
Pillar 03

Subsidized Book Purchases

Want to keep a title in your permanent home shelf? Purchase physical books at specially negotiated, lesser prices directly through the library.

Direct publisher partnerships ✓
Pillar 04

Free City Pickup Hubs

Skip expensive courier fees. Request borrowed or purchased books online and pick them up at no extra charge from partner hubs across Chattogram.

Zero shipping fee option ✓
Pillar 05

Rare Heritage of Akabir Ulama

We curate verified editions of major classical masterworks—from Imam Al-Ghazali, Ibn Kathir, and Shah Waliullah Dehlawi to revered modern thinkers like Ashraf Ali Thanwi and Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi.

Authenticated editions & historical treatises ✓
