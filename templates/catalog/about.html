Our Journey & Mission

About Youth Club Library

Preserving Wisdom in a Digital Era

The Youth Club Library is a dedicated, non-profit initiative established and managed by Youth Club Chittagong. Born out of a passion for nurturing knowledge, our mission is to build a bridge between traditional values and modern learning paradigms.

We are a thoughtful, modern, and welcoming space designed specifically for young minds to explore the profound richness of Islamic knowledge. By offering a carefully curated and selected collection of books across theology, history, biography, science, and moral philosophy, we hope to inspire intellectual growth, curiosity, and community alignment.
